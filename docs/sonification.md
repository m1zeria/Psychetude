## brain waves

brain waves are divided into the following types based on frequency:
  1. **delta** (0.5 to 4 Hz): slowest waves with highest amplitude. 
  occur during deep, dreamless sleep.
  2. **theta** (4 to 8 Hz): slow waves. occur during light sleep, 
  relaxation, daydreaming etc.
  3. **alpha** (8 to 12 Hz): medium waves. occur when awake, calm
  or generally relaxed with eyes closed.
  4. **beta** (12 to 30 Hz): fast waves. occur when wide awake and
  focused. too many cause stress and anxiety.
  5. **gamma** (30 to 100 Hz): the fastest waves. occur during high
  level thinking, intense focus and when multi-sensory percepts are
  being processed.
