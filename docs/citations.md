## mapping & sonification

**Barrass, S. & Kramer, G. (1999).** "Using sonification."
*Multimedia Systems*, 7(1), 23–31.
> foundational taxonomy; distinguishes audification from
> parameter-mapping sonification.
> supports: rationale.md — parameter mapping as a legitimate choice.
> [skimmed]

**Dubus, G. & Bresin, R. (2013).** "A systematic review of mapping
strategies for the sonification of physical quantities." *PLoS ONE*,
8(12), e82491.
> review of mapping strategies across sonification literature.
> supports: mapping.md — log-frequency is standard in this literature.
> [skimmed]

**Hermann, T., Hunt, A. & Neuhoff, J. G. (2011).** *The Sonification
Handbook.* Logos Publishing.
> reference work; chapters on perceptual foundations and design.
> supports: mapping.md — general framework.
> [skimmed]

**Kramer, G. et al. (1999).** "Sonification report: status of the
field and research agenda." *ICAD / NSF.*
> early community report; defines scope of the field.
> supports: rationale.md — historical context.
> [skimmed]

## pitch perception

**Attneave, F. & Olson, R. K. (1971).** "Pitch as a medium: a new
approach to psychophysical scaling." *American Journal of Psychology*,
84(2), 147–166.
> empirical evidence that pitch scales logarithmically with frequency.
> supports: mapping.md — the log-frequency choice.
> [to read]

**Stevens, S. S. (1957).** "On the psychophysical law." *Psychological
Review*, 64(3), 153–181.
> the classic treatment of logarithmic perception.
> supports: mapping.md — foundational.
> [to read]

**Zwicker, E. & Fastl, H. (1990).** *Psychoacoustics: Facts and
Models.* Springer.
> reference for jnds, masking, critical bands.
> supports: mapping.md — pitch discrimination limits.
> [skimmed]
